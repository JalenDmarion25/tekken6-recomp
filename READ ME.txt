TEKKEN 6 RECOMPILED WINDOWS BUILD

Copy this folder to Windows. Keep bin and game beside the launchers.
Each launcher offers internal 3D resolutions from lightest to heaviest GPU workload: 640x360, 768x432, and original 1024x576.
The chooser also enables a 512x512 size for the 1024x1024 offscreen target by default; this can soften shadows. Uncheck it to keep the original size.
Unsupported render cases fall back to the standard implementation.
Press F3 in game to show or hide the FPS and frame-time overlay.
Only unhandled crashes write reports under crashes/<run>/.
The launchers share user saves and persistent caches under this folder.
tekken6.exe starts the D3D12 ROV build (widest compatibility).
